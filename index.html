08 — ABOUT

The short version

I'm a Mechanical Engineering candidate focused on industrial automation, robotics, motion control, and manufacturing equipment support. The through-line of my work is simple: build the machine, then build the system that watches it.

That means ABB/Rexroth robot cells next to a self-hosted vLLM/llama.cpp inference stack, Klipper motion systems next to a Pi-supervised power policy engine, and CAD/CAM fabrication next to camera-based print failure detection. I'm comfortable reading technical manuals, writing procedures, training operators, and translating engineering requirements into shop-floor steps.

Target roles: industrial automation, manufacturing, robotics, and production/equipment engineering.
