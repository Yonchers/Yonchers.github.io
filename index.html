Local AI / Industry 4.0 server

02 | e3ng corexy

Ender 3 to CoreXY platform

Custom E3NG conversion, Klipper configuration, board integration, CAN toolhead communication, and calibration.

Controls mindset

  • Converted an Ender 3 Pro into a modern CoreXY motion platform using the E3NG open source project.
  • Self-sourced parts, assembled the frame, created wiring harnesses, and configured Klipper from scratch.
  • Used Raspberry Pi control, BigTreeTech controller hardware, CAN-style toolhead communication, Mainsail, Crowsnest, and probe calibration.
  • Built around a practical goal: reuse old hardware and prove it can become a faster, more capable printer using modern calibration methods.

03 | voron systems

Voron 2.4 printer build

Large-format, enclosed, high-speed printer assembly for technical materials and manufacturing-style rapid prototyping.

Detailed assembly

  • Printed parts in ABS, then moved toward ABS-GF15 after early fatigue issues to improve rigidity and vibration behavior.
  • Managed frame alignment, flying gantry assembly, squareness checks, electronics bay layout, DIN rail mounting, power distribution, and controller setup.
  • Worked with BTT Manta hardware, CAN toolhead boards, Klipper-based control, and custom toolhead/hotend modifications.
  • Built this as a quiet, enclosed, manufacturing-oriented printer capable of supporting advanced materials and larger prototyping needs.

06 | senior design

Hybrid hand-actuated espresso machine

ME190 senior design project focused on compact product design, pressure and thermal targets, CAD, drawing packages, and prototype communication.

Design targets

  • Design a compact hobby-grade espresso machine targeting approximately 9 bar / 130 psi brewing pressure and about 96 C water temperature.
  • Use mechanical advantage through a worm drive and lever arm to keep user actuation manageable.
  • Support food-grade material choices, cleanability, repairability, and a premium appearance.
  • Contributed to electronics optimization, power allocation, CAD assemblies, prototype printing, diagrams, and presentation support.
