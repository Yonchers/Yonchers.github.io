Overview

I turned an Ender 3 platform into a custom CoreXY machine.

The problem, my contribution, and the constraint that shaped the work.

Context

The problem I was solving

I wanted more than a cosmetic Ender upgrade. The goal was to use the donor machine as the basis for a CoreXY platform that could support higher-speed motion, engineering materials, and deeper controls work.

System

What I built

The conversion combined a new frame and belt path, custom toolhead packaging, Raspberry Pi control, SKR electronics, a U2C/CAN-style interface, sensors, camera, Klipper configuration, and a full calibration workflow.

Challenge

The hard part

The project crossed mechanical, electrical, Linux, firmware, and thermal boundaries. A symptom such as failed homing or bad first layers could come from alignment, wiring, network configuration, sensor behavior, or software settings.

Impact

Why it matters

The machine became a small automated workcell that I could assemble, commission, monitor, troubleshoot, and improve. Those are the same habits needed in larger manufacturing and automation systems.

Design decisions

Mechanical alignment, electronics, and firmware had to work as one system.

The tradeoffs and safeguards I used to move the project forward.

01

Treat alignment as a system problem

Frame squareness, belt routing, gantry movement, bed alignment, and toolhead stiffness were checked together instead of tuned in isolation.

02

Move calibration into a repeatable workflow

Input Shaper, bed mesh, homing, extrusion, thermal behavior, and repeatability checks became documented commissioning steps.

03

Use networked toolhead electronics carefully

CAN-style connectivity reduced the toolhead wiring burden but added Linux network and interface failure modes that had to be understood and recovered.

04

Build for service access

Controller, Pi, power, camera, probe, and toolhead connections were arranged so future diagnosis would not require dismantling the entire machine.

The result, the main lesson, and the next useful improvement.
